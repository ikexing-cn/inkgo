语言：en
题材：novel
= 设定
People｜Mara｜Mara repairs the harbor lamps and refuses to sail at night.
Places｜North Pier｜An abandoned pier beyond the lighthouse.
Rules｜｜The harbor closes at sunset.
= 卷：The Missing Light
卷纲：Mara traces a signal to the abandoned pier and chooses to leave home.
== Chapter 1: The Signal
章纲：Mara discovers a light beyond the closed harbor.
Mara counted three flashes beyond North Pier. The harbor had closed an hour ago, but someone was still out there. She took her father's lantern from its hook.

== Chapter 2: A Borrowed Boat
章纲：Mara asks a friend for passage.
The boat rocked against the steps. 'Don't wait for daylight,' Eli said. Mara tightened the rope and looked back at the lamps along the shore.

== Chapter 3: North Pier
章纲：The travelers find an unexpected message.
At North Pier, the light came from a broken window. Mara climbed the stairs one at a time. A letter lay beneath the lamp, addressed to someone who had vanished years before.

== Chapter 4: The Keeper
章纲：Mara learns who has been sending the signal.
The keeper opened the door before she knocked. He had watched her cross the water. 'Your father left something here,' he said, and handed her a small brass key.

== Chapter 5: Morning Tide
章纲：Mara decides to search beyond the harbor.
Morning arrived with a rising tide. Mara unlocked the cabinet and found a map of the outer islands. This time, she would leave before the lamps went out.
